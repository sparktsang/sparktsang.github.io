### Beyond Me

Measuring myself against Socrates, Mozart, and Shakespeare can make a short life feel painfully small. Still, I keep striving to become someone I could admire.  

Reading is one way I pursue that ambition. Circumstances, wealth, and status are not the measures I choose for a life. I care more about what I can cultivate: thought, character, ability, and the drive to act.  

I've loved reading since childhood, but I had a problem: I rarely finished the books I bought. So I began reading in bookstores, and finally managed to finish one book after another. Then came another problem: I forgot most of what I'd read. I began distilling the ideas worth keeping into notes. Over the years, that practice became 93 hand-crafted reading notes.  

I built this Library to give these personal treasures a lasting home. I hope they outlive me—not merely as records of what I read, but as ideas others can carry further. It would be my greatest honour if something here helped inspire a future Socrates, Mozart, or Shakespeare.  

To help these ideas travel beyond me, I designed the notes as more than static documents. I added audio deep dives for those who prefer listening, created infographics as a visual bonus for readers who reach the end, and invented “confluences”—an editorial format that bridges notes and articles, letting one idea lead into another.  

Some paths lead further still: into open editions where readers can challenge the text, propose corrections, and help improve it—with accepted contributions credited.  

If a note helps you see something differently, enjoy an idea, or discover a connection you might otherwise have missed, then it has already travelled beyond me. Perhaps that's the closest thing to immortality a reading note can offer.
