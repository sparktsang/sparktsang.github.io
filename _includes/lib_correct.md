### On Errors, AI, and Correction

I make these notes myself, but this library is not produced by human effort alone. Translation, proofreading, infographic creation and review, editorial writing, and quality control all involve substantial AI assistance. I also review the work myself—but neither human judgment nor AI is infallible.

Errors may therefore exist here: mistranslations, omissions, factual mistakes, typographical errors, visual inaccuracies, or errors in my own original notes. I do not assume that my error rate is lower than that of other authors, editors, translators, or AI systems.

What matters is what happens when an error is found.

The original Markdown sources are openly available on [GitHub][lib_github]{:target="_blank"}. Anyone may propose a correction through a public edit or pull request; corrections can also be [submitted by email][email]. I will review every correction I see. If I reject one, I will give a reason, and disagreement is welcome.

When a correction is accepted, I will credit the contributor on the page and link to a website or social profile of their choice.

I do not claim infallibility. I claim [openness to correction][phi]{:target="_blank"}.
